Summary. Bom Blast: Sort&Bounce is a single-player puzzle game that runs offline. There is no account system and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. We do not process payments and the game contains no in-app purchases. Your game progress and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your own device.

The current release does not display advertising and does not operate an analytics or crash-reporting service. The app is built with Google Play services and Firebase client libraries, which may collect limited technical and diagnostic data as described in section 4.

1. Scope

This policy explains what information the game Bom Blast: Sort&Bounce ("the App", "we", "us") stores or processes, why, who it may be shared with, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android version of the App distributed through Google Play under the package name com.playlab.bomblast.

This policy covers only the App. It does not cover Google Play itself, your device operating system, or any other software you may have installed.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any password.
  • We do not collect precise location or GPS data.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, SMS messages, call logs, photos, documents, camera or microphone.
  • We do not process payments. The App contains no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising or credit scoring.

3. Information stored on your device

The following data is written to the App's private storage area, which is protected by the Android application sandbox and is not readable by other apps. We do not upload this data to our own servers — we do not operate any game server.

DataPurpose
Audio and feedback settings: background music volume, sound effect volume, vibration on/off To remember your preferences between sessions
Gameplay state: current and unlocked levels, level results, in-game virtual coins and items To save and restore your progress and in-game economy

Android system backup

Android's built-in backup and device-transfer feature is enabled for this App. This means that the locally stored settings and progress described above may be included in a backup associated with your own Google account, and restored when you reinstall the App or set up a new device. That backup is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and is not accessible to us.

4. Third-party libraries and technical data

The App is built with Google Play services and Firebase client libraries. In the current release, advertising, product analytics, user authentication, cloud save and crash reporting are not activated: we do not display ads, we do not record gameplay analytics events, and we do not receive crash reports from your device.

Because these Google libraries are part of the application package, a limited amount of technical or diagnostic data — for example app version, device model, operating system version, or service-availability and integrity checks — may still be processed by Google as the provider of those platform services. Where this happens, Google acts as the data controller or processor for that processing under its own terms:

Any such data may be processed on Google servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. We do not sell data and we do not share data with data brokers or advertising networks.

If a future update activates advertising, analytics or crash reporting, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration before or together with that release, and — where required by law — ask for your consent inside the App.

6. Legal basis and purpose limitation

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies: data stored on your device is processed in order to provide the service you requested, namely playing the game. Technical data processed by the Google libraries described in section 4 is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in distributing and operating a functioning application, or on the basis of your consent where local law requires it.

We use data only for the purposes described in this policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is retained until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes your saved progress and settings from the device.
  • Android system backups are retained and deleted according to your Google account backup settings, which you control in your device settings.
  • Technical data processed by Google is retained according to Google's own retention policies for the relevant platform service.
  • We do not operate our own server and therefore keep no copy of your gameplay data.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete all local data: uninstall the App, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Bom Blast: Sort&Bounce → Storage → Clear data. This removes your saved progress and settings.
  • Exclude the App from backups: turn off backup for the App in your Android / Google account backup settings.
  • Turn off haptic feedback: use the in-game settings panel.
  • Access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability: because the App has no account system and we hold no directly identifying information, we normally have no data about you to locate. If you believe we hold data relating to you, contact us using the details in section 11 and we will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the United Kingdom this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Game progress and settings are kept in the App's private storage area, which is protected by the Android application sandbox. Any network requests made by the bundled Google libraries are transmitted over HTTPS. Please note that no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
