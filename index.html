Privacy Policy

Application: Roblock (Android package com.roblock.game)
Effective date: 2026-08-20  |  Last updated: 2026-08-20
Developer: Janet Wang  |  Contact: janet.yangwang@gmail.com

Summary: Roblock is a single-player offline game. It has no account system, no login, no in-app purchases and no third-party advertising SDK. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The only data leaving your device is anonymous, aggregated gameplay analytics sent to Google Analytics for Firebase.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Roblock mobile game ("the App", "we") collects, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and the choices available to you. It applies to the Android version of the App distributed through Google Play.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask you to register an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • We do not collect precise or GPS location.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, photos, files, camera or microphone.
  • We do not process payments and there are no in-app purchases.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising or credit scoring.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Data stored only on your device

The following data is written to the App's private storage on your device (localStorage / app sandbox files). It is not uploaded to us and is not readable by other apps:

DataPurpose
Game progress: unlocked and cleared levels, star ratings, failure counts, last played levelSave and restore your progress
In-game virtual resources: gold, fragments, energy, hero and turret upgrade levels, missions, patrol / idle timersGame economy and progression
Settings: background music, sound effects, haptics, left/right-hand layout, volume levelsRemember your preferences
A randomly generated player identifier (UUID v4, key roblock:player-id:v1)Distinguish one installation from another in analytics; it is generated on-device and is not derived from any hardware ID, advertising ID, phone number or account
Local analytics event log files (app writable path, folder roblock-analytics, capped at 2000 lines)Local diagnostics and gameplay tuning by the developer

All of this local data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear the App's data in Android system settings. Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled for the App (android:allowBackup="false").

3.2 Analytics data sent off the device

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase project roblcok-2d9d5) to understand how the game is played so we can balance difficulty and fix problems. Events are also sent to Google Analytics 4 through the Measurement Protocol endpoint https://www.google-analytics.com/mp/collect.

CategoryExamples of what is sent
App interactions / in-game eventsLevel started, level cleared, level failed, energy insufficient, upgrade card selected, rewarded-video placement shown or cancelled, save reset, event timestamp
Progression valuesLevel ID, segment index, virtual currency amounts, attempt counts, player level
Pseudonymous identifierThe on-device generated player identifier described in 3.1, and the Firebase app-instance ID generated by the Firebase SDK
Technical data collected automatically by the Firebase SDKApp version, device model, operating system version, language, country/region and coarse location inferred by Google from the IP address, first-open and session events

This data is pseudonymous. We do not attempt to re-identify individual users from it, and we do not combine it with any information that identifies you personally.

5. Legal basis and purpose limitation

Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, we process device-side game data to perform the service you requested (playing the game), and we process analytics data on the basis of our legitimate interest in measuring and improving the App, or on the basis of your consent where consent is required by local law. Where the app is distributed in jurisdictions requiring consent for analytics, the consent prompt shown in the App governs whether analytics are enabled.

6. Sharing and international transfer

We share data only with the service provider below. We do not sell data and we do not share it with data brokers or advertising networks.

RecipientRoleTheir policy
Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd (Google Analytics for Firebase) Analytics processor Firebase Privacy · Google Privacy Policy

Analytics data may be processed on Google servers outside your country, including in the United States. We may also disclose data if required by applicable law, legal process or a lawful government request.

7. Retention

  • On-device data: kept until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics data: retained in Google Analytics according to the retention period configured in the Firebase/GA4 console (default: 2 months for user-level data, up to 14 months maximum), after which it is deleted or aggregated by Google.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete all your data: uninstall the App, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Roblock → Storage → Clear data. This removes your save, settings, local analytics logs and the on-device player identifier.
  • Reset progress in-game: use the reset option in the game settings to clear level progress and virtual resources.
  • Turn off haptics: in the in-game settings panel.
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection, portability: because we hold no account and no directly identifying data, we generally cannot look you up. If you contact us at janet.yangwang@gmail.com and provide your in-app player identifier, we will use it to locate and delete the corresponding analytics records where technically feasible, and will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at janet.yangwang@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Security

Analytics requests are transmitted over HTTPS. Device-side save data is kept in the App's private storage area protected by the Android application sandbox. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced in the app store listing or inside the App. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
